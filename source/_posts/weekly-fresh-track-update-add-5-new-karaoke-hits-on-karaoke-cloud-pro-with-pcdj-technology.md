This is fantastic news for both new and existing subscribers — _KJs can count on at least 20 karaoke songs added each month!_

The karaoke tracks released into the cloud each week will consist of brand spanking new karaoke releases or older core-catalog karaoke tunes that have recently been re-licensed by Digitrax to include in Karaoke Cloud Pro. File quality will never waver, as many of the best karaoke brands are included: DigiTrax Karaoke (formerly Chartbuster), SBI, Sunfly, Stage Stars, DK and Mr. Entertainer.
