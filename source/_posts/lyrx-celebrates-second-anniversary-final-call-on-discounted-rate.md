## LYRX Celebrates Second Anniversary: Final Call on Discounted Rate!

[![LYRX is the best karaoke software](https://i0.wp.com/pcdj.com/wp-content/uploads/2019/08/lyrx-pricehike-cover.jpg?resize=706%2C321&ssl=1)](https://i0.wp.com/pcdj.com/wp-content/uploads/2019/08/lyrx-pricehike-cover.jpg?fit=706%2C360&ssl=1 "LYRX is the best karaoke software")

[LYRX](http://lyrxkaraoke.com/) may be the newest member of our PCDJ software lineup, but its quickly become one of the most popular with professional KJs and venues.

LYRX (Mac/Windows) just turned two, and over the last year we’ve witnessed major improvements to professional karaoke features that include singer/song history, managed singer rotation, ability to apply key changes in rotation, filler music player options, and more. LYRX has become a reliable karaoke show hosting product that rivals features found in much more expensive karaoke programs (and those products don’t even include FREE updates!).

If you were one of the lucky early adopters you’re still entitled to free updates and our industry-best customer service, and can hang your hat on taking a small risk on a little known product with big goals early on. We truly appreciate it.

If you’re new to LYRX — whelp, it’s time to [take it for a test spin](http://lyrxkaraoke.com/free-trial-2/) and see what all the noise is about. A modern, snappy karaoke software that’s disrupting the status quo…and we’re just getting this thing ramped up!
