I created this **"Introduction to Digital DJing"** live webinar presentation over 2 years ago. **Djing is as popular as ever**, and recent [**NAMM**](http://www.namm.org/) reports suggest as many kids today want to "wiki wiki (that's supposed to be a scratch sound)" as they want to be the next Zakk Wylde on the gitter.
